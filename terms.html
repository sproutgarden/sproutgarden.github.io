The app

Sprout is a habit tracker where every habit grows a plant. The free version includes the whole core experience with up to three habits and three custom rewards. Sprout Pro adds unlimited habits and rewards, a collection of rare plants and window views, deeper statistics and seasonal content.

Sprout Pro

  • Pro is sold through Apple's App Store as an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you buy.
  • The yearly subscription may start with a free trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, the subscription begins and Apple charges your Apple ID.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them in your Apple ID settings. Cancelling stops future charges; the current period runs to its end.
  • Payments, refunds and billing are handled by Apple under the App Store terms. Refund requests go to Apple.
  • When Pro ends, nothing you grew or bought is deleted. Three habits keep growing; the others wait in the Hall of habits with every check-in kept, and come back with Pro.

Sunlight

Sunlight is an in-app points system earned by checking in. It has no monetary value, cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money, and exists only inside the app.
