Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Sprout does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-up and no server of ours. Everything you do in Sprout, your habits, check-ins, plants, Sunlight and rewards, is stored only on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Your habits, their weekly goals and reminder times.
  • Your check-ins, the plants and pots they grew, and your 66-day counts.
  • Your Sunlight balance and the ledger of how it was earned and spent.
  • The name you type in onboarding (used only to greet you on the Today screen).
  • Your custom rewards and the treats you redeemed.

This data lives in the app's own storage on your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it. "Delete all data" in Settings erases it while keeping the app installed. Sprout does not use iCloud sync in this version, so nothing is copied to Apple's servers by the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics or crash reports through third parties.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or any advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any personal data, because we never receive any.
  • We do not use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Reminders

If you switch reminders on, Sprout asks iOS for permission to show local notifications. These are scheduled and shown entirely on your device. Turning reminders off in Settings removes them.

Purchases (Sprout Pro)

Sprout Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds your purchase history under its own privacy policy. Sprout only receives a confirmation from Apple that Pro is active, so it can unlock Pro features. We never see your payment details.

Children

Sprout is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects none.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if a future version adds optional iCloud sync, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the app will say so before anything leaves your device.

Contact

Questions about privacy: sproutgardensupport@gmail.com.
