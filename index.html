good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need an account?

No. Everything lives on your iPhone. No sign-up, no cloud, and none of your data is sent anywhere. (Sprout only downloads a small public file of letters from us.)

What if I miss a day?

Nothing dies and nothing resets. Your plant just has a little less time to bloom that week.

When does a new day start?

At 4 am, not midnight, so a late-night check-in still counts for the day you meant.

What happens when Pro ends?

Nothing is deleted. Three habits keep growing, the others wait in your Hall of habits with every check-in kept.

How do I cancel Pro?

Apple handles subscriptions: iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Sprout, Cancel Subscription. Do it at least 24 hours before renewal, and nothing you grew is ever deleted. Step by step on the support page.

Can I buy Sunlight?

No, and you never will. Sunlight is only earned by checking in.
