Sprout website

Files: index.html, privacy.html, terms.html, style.css, site.js, assets/, screens/

Before publishing, search and replace these placeholders in all three HTML files:
  APP_STORE_URL   -> your App Store link (e.g. https://apps.apple.com/app/id1234567890)
  SUPPORT_EMAIL   -> your support email
  SITE_URL        -> your site address (e.g. https://sprouthabits.app)
  LAST_UPDATED    -> a date, e.g. 3 October 2026
  DEVELOPER_NAME  -> your name as it appears in App Store Connect

Screenshots: replace the files in screens/ with real app screenshots, same names:
  shelf.jpg, detail.jpg, week.jpg, hall.jpg, rewards.jpg (portrait, any size; 1179x2556 from the iPhone 16 Pro simulator is ideal)
The Today screen and the Pro screen on the page are drawn live and need no screenshot.

Hosting: upload everything to GitHub Pages (repo root) or any static host. No build step.
